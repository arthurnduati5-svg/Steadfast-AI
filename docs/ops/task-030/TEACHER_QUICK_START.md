# Steadfast AI — Teacher Quick Start for Controlled Expansion

## Your Role in Expansion

As a teacher, you can view oversight items assigned to you. You cannot trigger control actions or view the full operations console.

## What You Can Do

- View assigned oversight items (safe summaries only)
- See whether students in your class have expanded access
- Contact admin if you notice issues

## What You Cannot Do

- View the operations dashboard
- Pause, resume, or rollback expansion
- View other teachers' oversight items
- View student private data or raw chat
- View reports
- Access kill switch controls

## Checking Assigned Oversight

1. Log in to Steadfast AI with your school account.
2. Navigate to the oversight section.
3. You will see only items assigned to you.
4. Each item shows a safe summary, severity, and status.
5. If no items are assigned, you will see an empty state.

## If You See Something Wrong

- Contact your school admin.
- Do not attempt to use control actions.
- Do not copy or share raw student data.
- Report any data exposure concerns immediately.

## Privacy Reminder

- Never share student chat logs.
- Never share private learner memory.
- Never share answer keys or teacher-only content.
- Never bypass school authentication.
